Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 10, 2026

Sensor ("we", "our", "the app") is a horary astrology application developed by Isovek. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Questions: The text of questions you ask within the app.
  • Account data: If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from Google.
  • City/Location: The city you select or your approximate location (used to calculate the astrological chart for your question).

Information collected automatically:

  • Analytics data: App usage patterns, screen views, and crash reports via Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Advertising ID: Google Advertising ID for personalized ads via Google AdMob.
  • Purchase data: Subscription and token purchase history via RevenueCat.
  • Device information: Device model, OS version, and app version for crash diagnostics.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate horary astrology interpretations for your questions.
  • To save your question history within the app.
  • To manage your account and subscription status.
  • To display relevant advertisements (free users).
  • To analyze app performance and fix crashes.
  • To improve our service and user experience.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect data:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, Analytics, Crashlytics — Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Advertising — Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat: Subscription & purchase management — Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In: Authentication — Privacy Policy

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Your question history is stored locally on your device.
  • Account data is stored securely in Firebase.
  • We use industry-standard encryption for data transmission (HTTPS/TLS).
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Your Rights

  • Delete your account: You can delete your account and all associated data from Settings → Danger Zone → Delete My Account.
  • Opt out of ads: Purchase a premium subscription to remove all advertisements.
  • Data access: Contact us to request a copy of your data.
  • Withdraw consent: You can revoke Google Sign-In permissions from your Google Account settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Sensor is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected data from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy within the app. Your continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
